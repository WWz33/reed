## Citation

reed implements published estimators. Cite the method papers when using the corresponding features:

| Feature | Literature |
|---|---|
| π | Nei & Li 1979 |
| dxy | Nei 1987 |
| Watterson θ | Watterson 1975 |
| Tajima's D | Tajima 1989 |
| Hudson Fst | Hudson 1992; Bhatia et al. 2013 |
| WC84 Fst | Weir & Cockerham 1984 |
| f-statistics (f2/f3/f4/D/f4-ratio) | Patterson et al. 2012 |
| PBS | Yi et al. 2010; PBSn1: Malaspinas et al. 2016 |
| SFS projection | Marth et al. 2004 |
| Missing-data handling for π/dxy/θ/D | Korunes & Samuk 2021 |
| HWE exact test | Wigginton et al. 2005 |
| LD (unphased r²) | Rogers & Huff 2009 |

The literature for each statistic also appears in `popg`'s `PREFIX.log` and its help text.
